The modulus a resilient layer springs with, for six materials a floating floor
is laid on: glass wool, two rock wools, polystyrene foam, polyurethane foam and
cork. A layer's dynamic stiffness per unit area is this modulus divided by its
thickness, so these rows give the stiffness of any thickness where the
[resilient layers](/phonometry/reference/catalogues/resilient-layers/) give it
for one. Every modulus is printed as a range and measured under a static load
of about 2 kPa, and the load matters: the same page says rock wool is about 20
per cent softer at 1 kPa and 30 per cent stiffer at 4.
