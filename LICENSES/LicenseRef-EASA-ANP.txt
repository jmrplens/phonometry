Applies to: the EASA/EUROCONTROL Aircraft Noise and Performance (ANP)
database tables bundled with phonometry under
src/phonometry/aircraft/data/anp/ (installed as phonometry/aircraft/data/anp/),
namely the files ending in .csv in that directory. Everything else in
phonometry is under the MIT licence in LICENSE.

Source: EASA ANP database, archive version 2.3 (released 14 October 2020),
published by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency at
https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/policy-support-and-research/aircraft-noise-and-performance-anp-data

Terms: the tables are reproduced under EASA's copyright notice,
https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/copyright-disclaimer, which reads:

    Reproduction is authorised, provided the source is acknowledged, save
    where otherwise stated.

The archive states no terms of its own, so that notice is the one that
applies.

Acknowledgement: the tables are the EASA/EUROCONTROL Aircraft Noise and
Performance (ANP) database, archive version 2.3, developed by the aircraft
manufacturers and reviewed by the US DOT Volpe Center, the US FAA, EASA and
EUROCONTROL. Anyone who passes the tables on keeps this acknowledgement with
them. The authoritative and complete dataset remains the one EASA publishes.

The provenance of the tables, what each file holds and how phonometry reads
them, is set out in src/phonometry/aircraft/data/anp/PROVENANCE.md.
