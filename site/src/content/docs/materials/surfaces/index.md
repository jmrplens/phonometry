---
title: "Surfaces measured in place"
description: "Surfaces that cannot be taken to a laboratory: the ISO 13472 in-situ absorption of a road pavement."
---

Some surfaces cannot be cut into a sample and carried to a reverberation room.
A road pavement is the standing case: it is characterised where it lies, with a
source and a microphone above it and a time window that separates the reflection
from everything else.

[In-situ road-surface absorption](/phonometry/materials/surfaces/road-absorption/)
implements both ISO 13472 methods, with the Adrienne window the standard
prescribes and the geometry corrections the measurement needs.
