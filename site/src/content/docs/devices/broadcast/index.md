---
title: "Broadcast"
description: "Programme loudness as broadcasting measures it: the EBU R 128 chain, its gating and the loudness range."
---

Broadcasting solved the loudness problem with a measurement rather than a
compressor: one number per programme, gated so that silence does not dilute it,
and a range that says how much the programme moves.

[Programme loudness (EBU R 128)](/phonometry/devices/broadcast/program-loudness/)
implements the ITU-R BS.1770 K-weighted chain with the EBU Tech 3341 gating and
the Tech 3342 loudness range, checked against the EBU's own test set.
